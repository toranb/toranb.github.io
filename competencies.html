Engineering Evaluation Framework

Emphasizes adaptability, pragmatism, and impact over pure technical depth

Core Competencies (11)
1. You can ideate & problem solve with others
Actively participates in cross-functional discussions and contributes valuable ideas that move problems forward
Facilitates collaborative problem-solving sessions that lead to better outcomes than individual work
2. You can estimate the impact of work
Asks insightful questions to understand the business value and opportunity cost before committing to work
Accurately identifies high-impact work that contributes to healthy margins and business outcomes
3. You can decompose problems
Breaks down complex problems into clear, manageable pieces that the team can execute on
Identifies dependencies and sequences work effectively to maintain momentum
4. You hammer and negotiate scope down
Challenges requirements to find the smallest valuable increment that serves customers
Protects the team from overcommitment by negotiating scope to focus on what matters most
5. You can deliver imperfect code
Ships "good enough" solutions quickly when speed-to-market matters more than perfection
Makes conscious tradeoffs between code quality and learning velocity, especially pre-product-market fit
6. You deliver consistently
Maintains predictable velocity and reliably follows through on commitments
Ships work frequently in a steady cadence that builds trust with stakeholders
7. You value fast feedback
Proactively seeks feedback from users, peers, and stakeholders throughout the development process
Builds feedback loops into their work (testing, monitoring, user research) to validate assumptions quickly
8. You learn from mistakes, yours and others
Openly shares their own mistakes and learnings with the team to prevent repeated errors
Actively learns from others' experiences and applies those lessons to their work
9. You can take feedback from end users and surface, prioritize, build and deliver iteratively
Engages directly with end users to understand their needs and translates feedback into actionable work
Delivers iteratively, incorporating user feedback into each cycle to improve outcomes
10. You know the benefits and the tradeoffs
Articulates the pros and cons of technical decisions clearly to help the team make informed choices
Balances going deeper technically vs. moving lean to learn, choosing appropriately for the context
11. You build throw away tools/scripts that accelerate you when it makes sense
Creates pragmatic automation and tooling when the time investment clearly pays off
Shows good judgment about when to build tools vs. doing things manually
Bonus Competencies (4)
Bonus: Inspires people
Brings enthusiasm and optimism that motivates others to stretch and take ownership
Demonstrates excellence that others want to emulate and learn from
Bonus: Helps the team deliver
Actively unblocks teammates and shares knowledge to accelerate team velocity
Improves team processes and practices that benefit everyone
Bonus: Casts vision to help people see more and before
Anticipates future challenges and opportunities, helping the team prepare proactively
Communicates a compelling vision of the future that aligns and energizes the team
Bonus: Seeks to stretch both technically and relationally
Actively develops both technical skills and interpersonal/leadership capabilities
Seeks growth opportunities outside their comfort zone in multiple dimensions
